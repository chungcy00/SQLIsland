## kp19-01 零售：净销售额与门店汇总

### 概念
有效订单为 completed；净销售额是明细数量×成交单价减整行折扣，不能使用商品标价代替。

### 语法
```sql
SUM(quantity*unit_price-discount_amount)
```

### 参数说明
- `quantity`：销售数量
- `discount_amount`：整行折扣

### 原始数据

**retail_orders**

| order_id | store_id | customer_id | order_date | status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 2026-01-01 | completed |
| 2 | 2 | 2 | 2026-01-02 | cancelled |
| 3 | 1 | 1 | 2026-02-01 | completed |

**order_items**

| order_item_id | order_id | product_id | quantity | unit_price | discount_amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 100 | 10 |
| 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 100 | 0 |
| 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 50 | 0 |

**stores**

| store_id | store_name |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 古晋 |
| 2 | 美里 |
| 3 | 诗巫 |

### MySQL 示例
```sql
SELECT s.store_id,COALESCE(SUM(i.quantity*i.unit_price-i.discount_amount),0) AS net_sales FROM stores s LEFT JOIN retail_orders o ON s.store_id=o.store_id AND o.status='completed' LEFT JOIN order_items i ON o.order_id=i.order_id GROUP BY s.store_id ORDER BY s.store_id;
```

### 执行过程
1. 先限制有效订单
2. 连接明细
3. 计算净额
4. 门店1合计340，其他门店0

### 执行结果
| store_id | net_sales |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 340 |
| 2 | 0 |
| 3 | 0 |

验证状态：SQLite实际执行；原生 MySQL 未验证。

### 使用场景
门店销售总额；延伸至类别贡献率、分组筛选、月度增长及店内前三商品

### 用法辨析
订单金额需按明细汇总；不能把订单条数当商品销量。

### 常见错误
错误：包含 cancelled 订单

修正：在连接条件限定 completed

### 注意事项
本课堂 retail_orders 对应正式 retail.orders；金额为整数演示。

## kp19-02 库存：快照与周转率

### 概念
库存是时点数量，销售出库是期间流量。周转率使用期间销售出库成本除期初期末平均库存成本。

### 语法
```sql
sales_cost / NULLIF((opening_value+closing_value)/2.0,0)
```

### 参数说明
- `opening_value`：期初成本库存
- `closing_value`：期末成本库存

### 原始数据

**stock_snapshots**

| snapshot_id | product_id | warehouse_id | snapshot_date | quantity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 2026-01-01 | 100 |
| 2 | 1 | 1 | 2026-01-31 | 60 |
| 3 | 2 | 1 | 2026-01-31 | 20 |

**stock_movements**

| movement_id | product_id | warehouse_id | movement_date | movement_type | quantity | unit_cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 2026-01-15 | sale_out | 40 | 10 |

### MySQL 示例
```sql
WITH s AS (SELECT SUM(CASE WHEN snapshot_date='2026-01-01' THEN quantity*10 ELSE 0 END) AS opening_value,SUM(CASE WHEN snapshot_date='2026-01-31' THEN quantity*10 ELSE 0 END) AS closing_value FROM stock_snapshots WHERE product_id=1 AND warehouse_id=1), m AS (SELECT SUM(quantity*unit_cost) AS sales_cost FROM stock_movements WHERE product_id=1 AND warehouse_id=1 AND movement_type='sale_out' AND movement_date>='2026-01-01' AND movement_date<'2026-02-01') SELECT ROUND(2.0*sales_cost/NULLIF(opening_value+closing_value,0),2) AS turnover FROM s CROSS JOIN m;
```

### 执行过程
1. 期初1000、期末600
2. 销售出库成本400
3. 均值800
4. 周转率0.5

### 执行结果
| turnover |
| --- |
| 0.5 |

验证状态：SQLite实际执行；原生 MySQL 未验证。

### 使用场景
库存周转；延伸至积压、低库存、滞销和库存金额

### 用法辨析
不能用销售收入除成本库存；不能汇总多个日期库存作为期末库存。

### 常见错误
错误：把所有出库当销售出库

修正：仅使用定义的销售出库类型

### 注意事项
课堂明确固定单位成本10，快照均存在；正式项目缺快照不得默认为0。

### 兼容性
直接兼容。仅在本示例数据与语义范围验证；不代表全部 MySQL 语法兼容。

## kp19-03 电商：复购率和客户粒度

### 概念
在指定期间按有效订单统计每客户购买次数，复购客户至少2次；分母为至少购买一次的客户。

### 语法
```sql
COUNT(*) 按 customer_id 分组；SUM(CASE WHEN n>=2 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)/COUNT(*)
```

### 参数说明
- `n`：有效订单数

### 原始数据

**commerce_orders**

| order_id | customer_id | order_date | status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 2026-01-01 | completed |
| 2 | 1 | 2026-02-01 | completed |
| 3 | 2 | 2026-02-01 | completed |
| 4 | 3 | 2026-02-02 | cancelled |

### MySQL 示例
```sql
WITH c AS (SELECT customer_id,COUNT(*) AS n FROM commerce_orders WHERE status='completed' AND order_date>='2026-01-01' AND order_date<'2026-03-01' GROUP BY customer_id) SELECT COUNT(*) AS buyers,SUM(CASE WHEN n>=2 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS repeat_buyers,ROUND(100.0*SUM(CASE WHEN n>=2 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)/NULLIF(COUNT(*),0),2) AS repeat_pct FROM c;
```

### 执行过程
1. 过滤取消订单
2. 客户1两笔、客户2一笔
3. 复购1人/购买2人

### 执行结果
| buyers | repeat_buyers | repeat_pct |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 1 | 50.0 |

验证状态：SQLite实际执行；原生 MySQL 未验证。

### 使用场景
复购率；延伸至客单价、消费分层、首购次月留存、消费排名

### 用法辨析
复购率不是复购订单数/订单数；留存需要明确首购期间。

### 常见错误
错误：按订单明细数判断购买次数

修正：按订单唯一编号统计

### 注意事项
commerce_orders 对应正式 ecommerce.orders；付款与明细先各自聚合。

### 兼容性
直接兼容。仅在本示例数据与语义范围验证；不代表全部 MySQL 语法兼容。

## kp19-04 财务：截止日未付金额

### 概念
未付金额为发票金额减截至统计日的累计付款；先按发票聚合付款。

### 语法
```sql
invoice.amount - COALESCE(paid,0)
```

### 参数说明
- `paid`：截至日累计支付金额

### 原始数据

**invoices**

| invoice_id | account_id | invoice_date | due_date | amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 2026-01-01 | 2026-01-31 | 100 |
| 2 | 1 | 2026-01-02 | 2026-02-01 | 200 |

**payments**

| payment_id | invoice_id | payment_date | amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 2026-01-10 | 40 |
| 2 | 1 | 2026-02-02 | 60 |

### MySQL 示例
```sql
WITH p AS (SELECT invoice_id,SUM(amount) AS paid FROM payments WHERE payment_date<'2026-02-01' GROUP BY invoice_id) SELECT i.invoice_id,i.amount-COALESCE(p.paid,0) AS outstanding FROM invoices i LEFT JOIN p ON i.invoice_id=p.invoice_id WHERE i.invoice_date<'2026-02-01' ORDER BY i.invoice_id;
```

### 执行过程
1. 截至1月末仅付款40
2. 发票1未付60
3. 发票2未付200

### 执行结果
| invoice_id | outstanding |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 60 |
| 2 | 200 |

验证状态：SQLite实际执行；原生 MySQL 未验证。

### 使用场景
发票未付；延伸至费用、收入增长、异常金额与预算差异

### 用法辨析
账龄与未付金额不同；发票不等于现金支出。

### 常见错误
错误：计入截止日之后的付款

修正：在付款汇总内限制截止日

### 注意事项

## kp19-06 采购：订购与收货差异

### 概念
收货可能分批，必须在采购明细粒度汇总实际收货，再与订购数量比较。

### 语法
```sql
quantity_ordered - COALESCE(received,0)
```

### 参数说明
- `received`：截至日累计收货数量

### 原始数据

**purchase_items**

| po_item_id | po_id | product_id | quantity_ordered | unit_price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 10 | 8 |
| 2 | 1 | 2 | 5 | 20 |

**receipts**

| receipt_id | po_item_id | receipt_date | quantity_received |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 2026-01-10 | 4 |
| 2 | 1 | 2026-01-12 | 5 |

### MySQL 示例
```sql
WITH r AS (SELECT po_item_id,SUM(quantity_received) AS received FROM receipts WHERE receipt_date<'2026-02-01' GROUP BY po_item_id) SELECT i.po_item_id,i.quantity_ordered-COALESCE(r.received,0) AS remaining FROM purchase_items i LEFT JOIN r ON i.po_item_id=r.po_item_id ORDER BY i.po_item_id;
```

### 执行过程
1. 明细1累计收货9，剩1
2. 明细2无收货，剩5

### 执行结果
| po_item_id | remaining |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 5 |

验证状态：SQLite实际执行；原生 MySQL 未验证。

订单收货差异；延伸至采购成本、供应商履约、延误及发票核对

### 用法辨析
每笔收货不是完整订单；到齐日期应依据累计数量达到订购量。

### 常见错误

修正：使用 LEFT JOIN 及 COALESCE

### 注意事项

## kp19-07 零售：类别销售表现

## kp19-08 零售：月度净销售增长率

### 概念
先计算按月净额，再在连续月份序列中读取上月金额；缺失上月或上月为零时增长率不可定义。

### 语法
```sql
LAG(net_sales) OVER(ORDER BY month)
```

### 参数说明
- `net_sales`：有效订单净销售额
- `month`：连续年月键

### 原始数据

**retail_orders**

| order_id | store_id | customer_id | order_date | status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 2026-01-01 | completed |
| 2 | 2 | 2 | 2026-01-02 | cancelled |
| 3 | 1 | 1 | 2026-02-01 | completed |

**order_items**

| order_item_id | order_id | product_id | quantity | unit_price | discount_amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 100 | 10 |
| 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 100 | 0 |
| 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 50 | 0 |

### MySQL 示例
```sql
WITH a AS (SELECT DATE_FORMAT(o.order_date,'%Y-%m') AS month,SUM(i.quantity*i.unit_price-i.discount_amount) AS net_sales FROM retail_orders o JOIN order_items i ON o.order_id=i.order_id WHERE o.status='completed' GROUP BY DATE_FORMAT(o.order_date,'%Y-%m')), p AS (SELECT month,net_sales,LAG(net_sales) OVER(ORDER BY month) AS prev FROM a) SELECT month,net_sales,ROUND(100.0*(net_sales-prev)/NULLIF(prev,0),2) AS growth_pct FROM p ORDER BY month;
```

### 执行过程
1. 按月聚合1月190、2月150
2. 取上一月190
3. 计算(150−190)/190

### 执行结果
| month | net_sales | growth_pct |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-01 | 190 | NULL |
| 2026-02 | 150 | -21.05 |

验证状态：SQLite实际执行；原生 MySQL 未验证。

### 使用场景
分析月销售变动幅度

### 用法辨析
环比对比上月；同比对比去年同月。

### 常见错误
错误：直接比较单笔订单计算每月增长率

修正：先按月汇总再使用 LAG

### 注意事项
课堂仅两个月且连续；正式库须构建完整月份轴，题目明确缺失与无交易口径。

### 兼容性
转换执行。需要明确转换或原生环境验证。

本地转换示例：
```sql
WITH a AS (SELECT strftime('%Y-%m',o.order_date) AS month,SUM(i.quantity*i.unit_price-i.discount_amount) AS net_sales FROM retail_orders o JOIN order_items i ON o.order_id=i.order_id WHERE o.status='completed' GROUP BY strftime('%Y-%m',o.order_date)), p AS (SELECT month,net_sales,LAG(net_sales) OVER(ORDER BY month) AS prev FROM a) SELECT month,net_sales,ROUND(100.0*(net_sales-prev)/NULLIF(prev,0),2) AS growth_pct FROM p ORDER BY month;
```

## kp19-09 零售：各店前三畅销商品

### 概念
销售额榜需要先汇总门店与商品净额，窗口排名后再限定名次。

### 语法
```sql
ROW_NUMBER() OVER(PARTITION BY store_id ORDER BY net_sales DESC,product_id)
```

### 参数说明
- `store_id`：排名分区
- `product_id`：并列时用于稳定排序

### 原始数据

**retail_orders**

| order_id | store_id | customer_id | order_date | status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 2026-01-01 | completed |
| 2 | 2 | 2 | 2026-01-02 | cancelled |
| 3 | 1 | 1 | 2026-02-01 | completed |

**order_items**

| order_item_id | order_id | product_id | quantity | unit_price | discount_amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 100 | 10 |
| 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 100 | 0 |
| 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 50 | 0 |

### MySQL 示例
```sql
WITH a AS (SELECT o.store_id,i.product_id,SUM(i.quantity*i.unit_price-i.discount_amount) AS net_sales FROM retail_orders o JOIN order_items i ON o.order_id=i.order_id WHERE o.status='completed' GROUP BY o.store_id,i.product_id), r AS (SELECT a.*,ROW_NUMBER() OVER(PARTITION BY store_id ORDER BY net_sales DESC,product_id) AS rn FROM a) SELECT store_id,product_id,net_sales,rn FROM r WHERE rn<=3 ORDER BY store_id,rn;
```

### 执行过程
1. 在门店内按商品汇总

## kp19-35 门店综合经营异常看板

### 业务要求

2025年第四季度线下门店，保留全部门店。输出净销售、毛利率、目标达成率、退款率及净销售RANK排名。以所有门店汇总后的加权指标为比较基准，不能平均各店比率。三项复核标记分别为毛利率低于整体毛利率1个百分点、达成率低于整体达成率的80%、退款率高于整体退款率的125%。整体达成率仅纳入目标金额为正的门店，分子也使用这些门店的净销售。退款率=退款额/退款前折后销售额。基准或门店比率分母为0时为NULL，该项不计标记。标记使用未舍入值，输出比率保留2位小数。按标记数降序、门店编号升序，排名并列保留空位。该表是相对经营复核名单，不代表公司已达到年度预算。

### 参考查询

```sql

  SELECT store_id, SUM(net_cents) net, SUM(cost_cents) cost, SUM(refund_cents) refund
  FROM v_sales_lines
  WHERE channel='store' AND order_date BETWEEN '2025-10-01' AND '2025-12-31'
  GROUP BY store_id
), b AS (
  SELECT store_id, SUM(target_cents) target FROM sales_targets
  WHERE month_key BETWEEN '2025-10' AND '2025-12' GROUP BY store_id
), p AS (
  SELECT s.store_id, COALESCE(a.net,0) net, COALESCE(a.cost,0) cost,
    COALESCE(a.refund,0) refund, b.target,
    100.0*(a.net-a.cost)/NULLIF(a.net,0) margin,
    100.0*COALESCE(a.net,0)/NULLIF(b.target,0) attainment,
    100.0*a.refund/NULLIF(a.net+a.refund,0) refund_rate
  FROM stores s LEFT JOIN a ON a.store_id=s.store_id LEFT JOIN b ON b.store_id=s.store_id
), benchmark AS (
  SELECT 100.0*SUM(net-cost)/NULLIF(SUM(net),0) margin,
    100.0*SUM(CASE WHEN target>0 THEN net ELSE 0 END)/NULLIF(SUM(CASE WHEN target>0 THEN target ELSE 0 END),0) attainment,
    100.0*SUM(refund)/NULLIF(SUM(net+refund),0) refund_rate FROM p
), z AS (
  SELECT p.*,
    CASE WHEN p.margin<benchmark.margin-1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END
    +CASE WHEN p.attainment<benchmark.attainment*.8 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END
    +CASE WHEN p.refund_rate>benchmark.refund_rate*1.25 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END flags,
    RANK() OVER(ORDER BY net DESC) sales_rank FROM p CROSS JOIN benchmark
```

## kp19-60 供应商多指标综合评价

### 业务要求

2025年到期采购行；至少10行；准时足量率40%、完整率25%、质量合格率20%、合同价格达标率15%（实际采购价≤合同价行数/到期行数）；质量分母为0时评分NULL；输出各指标和总分，按总分降序。

### 参考查询

```sql
WITH a AS (SELECT v.supplier_id,COUNT(*) n,100.0*SUM(CASE WHEN v.on_time>=v.quantity_ordered THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)/COUNT(*) ot,100.0*SUM(CASE WHEN v.accepted>=v.quantity_ordered THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)/COUNT(*) fill,100.0*SUM(accepted)/NULLIF(SUM(received),0) quality,100.0*SUM(CASE WHEN v.unit_price_cents<=c.unit_price_cents THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)/COUNT(*) price_ok FROM v_po_lines v JOIN purchase_order_items i ON i.po_item_id=v.po_item_id JOIN supplier_contracts c ON c.contract_id=i.contract_id WHERE v.promised_date BETWEEN '2025-01-01' AND '2025-12-31' GROUP BY v.supplier_id) SELECT supplier_id,n due_lines,ROUND(ot,2) ontime_pct,ROUND(fill,2) complete_pct,ROUND(quality,2) quality_pct,ROUND(price_ok,2) contract_pct,ROUND(.4*ot+.25*fill+.2*quality+.15*price_ok,2) score FROM a WHERE n>=10 ORDER BY score DESC,supplier_id;
```

### 执行思路

1. 计算四项百分比
2. 保留充足样本
3. 加权评分
4. 排序

### 易错点

不说明权重、样本门槛和缺失指标处理会使评分不可解释。
